HOW TO PLAY

Link Matching Rules

Select two matching computer components. They can be linked if the line connecting them makes **no more than 2 turns** (maximum 3 straight line segments) through empty spaces or around the grid edge.

Combo System

Match pairs rapidly to build your Combo Multiplier up to **x5** for massive bonus scores!

Stage Gravity Mechanics

Starting from Stage 2, matching tiles will trigger grid shifts (Gravity Down, Left, Up, Right) to mix up the board layout!

Power-Ups

Hint: Highlights a valid matching pair.
Shuffle: Re-arranges remaining tiles when stuck.
